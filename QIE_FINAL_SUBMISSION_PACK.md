## Longer Description

```text
QIE Growth Copilot helps QIE builders understand wallet readiness without manually reading raw chain data. A user can open the QIE demo, run a live QIE Testnet wallet check, and receive a concise AI growth brief with balance, transaction activity, latest block verification, readiness score, risk status, and recommended next actions.

The project also includes a QIEGrowthProof smart contract deployed on QIE Testnet. A sample generated brief hash is recorded on-chain, giving reviewers a verifiable proof trail through the QIE explorer.

The goal is to make wallet intelligence easier for QIE ecosystem teams: raw wallet data in, AI-guided growth brief out, and proof anchored on QIE Testnet.
```
